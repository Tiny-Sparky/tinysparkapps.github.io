What is Point Me?

Point Me is a simple and fun tool that helps you make quick decisions. Whether you're choosing who starts a game, picking a direction, or selecting a random option, Point Me gives you an instant answer with a clean and joyful interface.

Features

  • 🎯 Instant pointing animation
  • 🎨 Clean and colorful interface
  • ⚡ Zero learning curve
  • 📱 Works offline
  • 🔄 Randomized and fair
  • 👆 One‑tap usage
